A book chapter on the study I was involved in during an internship in the lab of Philippe Moullier at INSERM Nantes (under the direct supervision of Jean-Baptiste Dupont).
The only blind study I was involved in: I worked only with numbered groups until the very end of my internship.
Upon presenting the results in front of a committee, I unveiled the identity by opening (for the first time!) an envelope identifying which mice had received the treatment, which were the control groups.
This was an extraordinary opportunity to work against some of the unconscious biases we have as scientists when analyzing results.
And it turns out I was wrong in my assumptions! What I thought for sure was the control group was not, because our treatment was inducing unexpected toxicity.
