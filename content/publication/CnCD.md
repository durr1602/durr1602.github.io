By far the biggest project to which I've contributed.
- The initial setup: run a deep-mutational scanning (DMS) experiment on two orthologous proteins and compare.
- The model: the cytosine deaminase, a fungal enzyme in the pyrimidine salvage pathway.
- The question: if we introduce the same mutation in the two orthologs, will they behave the same way?

We compare specifically the mutational landscapes of 5-FC resistance, which in this context is assumed to be caused by loss of function.
The why is particularly tricky, and makes for a great story. We show a few examples of molecular mechanisms underlying epistasis.
