A study initially designed to saturate the mutational landscape of 5-FC resistance, ultimately finding a hotspot in FUR1, an enzyme responsible for 5-FC to 5-FU conversion. 5-FU cross-feeding, an unforeseen consequence of the experimental design, led to a selection of FUR1 LOF mutants. This is conceptually analogous to the "bystander effect" observed in cancer therapy: when targeting tumor cells by transfecting with FCY1-FUR1, untransfected tumor cells can still be killed by 5-FU diffusing from transfected cells.
