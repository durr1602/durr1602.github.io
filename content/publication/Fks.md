DMS of Fks hotspots to map the mutational landscape of echinocandin resistance. This was a tremendous endeavour, initially including all pairwise combinations of single amino acid substitutions for all four hotspots across both Fks paralogs, screening with the three most widely used echinocandins in the clinic. This was also an important collaboration with Patrick, resulting in impressive analyses including molecular dynamics simulations, as well as SILCS-based docking. Unfortunately, we went through several major rounds of revision, part of the computational models being questioned... In the end, the story is much more concise. Even though it now likely includes 10% of the work that we did, it's still pretty cool. It's probably more easily digestible than it could ever have been and we learned a lot along the way.
