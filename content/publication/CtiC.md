One of the longest studies I've been a part of (from start to publication).
Part of this was actually included in my PhD thesis back in 2020.
The biggest issues we had was:
- We had no structural insight (AlphaFold wasn't out yet when we started and it took a few updates to get the first reliable prediction)
- Several experiments such as EMSA failed because the protein was very difficult to work with

Many people had to pitch in to do some more experiments until we were finally able to characterize a very cool and novel mechanism of fertility inhibition.
