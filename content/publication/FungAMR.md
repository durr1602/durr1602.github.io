FungAMR is a really cool project, made possible by [EvoFunPath](https://evofunpath.ibis.ulaval.ca/), a pan-canadian training program on the evolution of fungal pathogens. It is essentially a curated database of mutations conferring antifungal resistance. It was an impressive collaborative effort, everyone (PIs, students, postdocs and other researchers) pitching in to read the literature all the way to the 1980s. This took coordination but the effort didn't stop there. The curated data were then analyzed, and a bioinfo tool, [ChroQueTas](https://github.com/nmquijada/ChroQueTas) was even developed to help researchers in the field.
